# Title
## Election Results & Voter Behavior Analysis in Kenya (2002–2022): A Data-Driven Study of Voting Patterns, Turnout, and Political Trends
________________________________________
# 1. Background
Kenya has conducted five general elections since the introduction of multiparty democracy in the modern era (2002, 2007, 2013, 2017, and 2022). These elections have significantly influenced the country's political landscape and have exhibited changing voter preferences, turnout, regional voting patterns, and party dominance.
Analyzing historical election data using modern data analytics techniques can provide valuable insights into how demographic, socio-economic, and regional factors influence voting behavior. Such findings can help researchers, policymakers, journalists, election observers, and political analysts better understand electoral dynamics and democratic participation. IEBC reports also show substantial growth in registered voters over this period, from about 10.45 million in 2002 to over 22 million in 2022. 
________________________________________
# 2. Problem Statement
Although Kenya has held multiple democratic elections, there is limited publicly available analytical work that integrates election results, voter turnout, demographic indicators, and socio-economic variables into one comprehensive analytical framework.
Most election reporting focuses only on winners and losers, leaving unanswered questions such as:
•	Why do some counties consistently vote for particular political coalitions? 
•	Which factors influence voter turnout? 
•	How has voter behavior changed over time? 
•	Are there identifiable regional voting trends? 
•	Can historical voting patterns help predict future elections? 
A data-driven approach can answer these questions using statistical analysis and visualization.
________________________________________
# 3. Research Questions
The study seeks to answer the following questions:
1.	How has voter turnout changed from 2002 to 2022? 
2.	Which counties consistently support particular political parties? 
3.	Which counties experience the highest and lowest voter turnout? 
4.	Does population size influence voter turnout? 
5.	What relationship exists between voter registration and actual turnout? 
6.	How has party dominance changed over time? 
7.	Which demographic and socio-economic indicators correlate with election outcomes? 
8.	Can historical election data predict future voting trends?
